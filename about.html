Hello!

From teaching myself 3D animation as a kid to hand-coding Neopets profiles, I've always been drawn to both the craft of making things and the systems behind them.

Product design is where that instinct lives. I love the part of design that nobody notices but everybody feels, where simplifying something complex is the hardest and most rewarding thing you can do.

Outside of work you can find me,

  • Rating films on Letterboxd
  • Obsessing over vintage Danish furniture
  • Practicing my Chinese in local language meetups

Want to chat? Reach me on LinkedIn or via email.

Design Philosophy

  1. Constraints are a creative tool, not a limitation. They sharpen thinking and often lead to better solutions.
  2. Data informs judgment, but does not replace it. Good design requires knowing when to look beyond what the numbers say.
  3. Inspiration is everywhere. The best ideas often come from unexpected connections between unrelated experiences.
